## New atlas illustrations

- `muscle-front-atlas.png`, `muscle-back-atlas.png`, and `internal-organs-atlas.png` were generated specifically for this app with Nano Banana Pro, then visually checked for major layout plausibility, correct view direction, limb count, and absence of rendered labels/watermarks.
- These are stylized educational illustrations, not clinical-grade anatomical references. The markers are approximate group-level touch targets; the separate 317-fragment interactive map is the finer-selection layer. No image label is used as medical content.
